## Plan: Jump the open page, then move the mark

### What went wrong

On The Hollow Men the first cue was right. The browser opened on the phrase, one small nudge settled it, and the box was the eyes and the sunlight on the broken column.

The later cues walked an already-open page by about seventy percent of the window. That stride is for a long document. On this poem it jumped over the rose, and that cue was given up. The second box landed thirty-nine pixels lower in the same column, after the page had already jumped most of a screen. The Shadow was marked in the last seconds, still in that column, after the page had been thrown about.

The tint is a place on the glass. A scroll slides the poem out from under it, so a box that barely moves on the screen is sitting on the wrong lines.

### The change

A follower that already has the page does what the first cue did.

1. The speech-held tint comes down before the page moves. `concealHighlight` hides that mark and keeps the box. The voice keeps going. Her arms come down with the tint.
2. On the web, the open tab is sent to a text fragment for the cue's phrase. No new window. The fresh screenshot is what decides. The screen-text memory from before the jump is not consulted, because it still describes the old viewport.
3. The crop is checked. When it passes, the new box goes up and is held until the utterance ends. The mark and the lines change together.
4. If the fragment does not land, the walk uses a short step, a quarter of the window, and reads again. A cold locate, including the first cue, still walks at about seventy percent of the window.
5. A miss leaves the tint down. The follower logs the matched words when a box is shown.

PDF, a terminal, and the editor have no text fragment. They still drop the tint before the search and raise it only after the crop passes. Their walk is unchanged.

### Out of scope

A long page such as The Waste Land stays a later stress test. This does not change the stride of a cold locate, the sentence clock, or the heat map.
